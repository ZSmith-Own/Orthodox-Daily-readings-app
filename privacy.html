Privacy Policy

Orthodox Bible & Daily Readings · Last updated: September 3, 2026

The short version: This app does not require an account and does not collect, store, or share your personal information. Your notes, bookmarks, and settings stay on your own device. The app connects to the internet for only two things — loading its typefaces and fetching the daily readings — and neither feature sends any personal data about you.

1. Who we are

Orthodox Bible & Daily Readings (the “app”) is an independent devotional reading application. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any church, jurisdiction, or Bible publisher. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information stored on your device

When you use the app, the following is saved locally on your device only, using your browser/app storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it:

  • Bookmarks and personal notes you create
  • Reading-plan progress
  • Display preferences (font size, light/dark theme, last position)

This information remains on your device until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app, at which point it is deleted. There is no cloud backup and no sync between devices.

3. Information sent when you use online features

The app’s Scripture text and commentary are built in and work offline. Two features require an internet connection, and — as with any internet request — the outside service involved will receive your device’s IP address and basic technical details (such as the type of device or browser). We do not add any identifier of our own, and no account, name, email, or location is sent.

FeatureService contactedWhat is sent
Daily lectionary readings (“Daily” tab and the readings strip) orthocal.info Only the calendar date being viewed, as part of the request address. No personal data.
App typefaces (fonts) Google Fonts (Google LLC) A standard request for the font files. No personal data.

In addition, whoever hosts the app’s files and the operating-system app store may keep standard, automatic server or download logs (for example IP address and time of request) as a normal part of delivering software over the internet. We do not control those logs and do not use them to identify you.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect or store your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies for tracking.
  • We do not create user accounts or profiles.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, files, or precise location.

5. Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on request.

6. Your choices and control

  • You can use nearly all of the app — Scripture, commentary, bookmarks, and notes — completely offline.
  • You can delete everything the app has stored at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.
  • To avoid the two online requests above, simply keep the device offline; the built-in content still works.

7. Third-party services

The two services the app contacts have their own privacy practices, which we do not control:

  • Google Fonts — policies.google.com/privacy
  • orthocal.info — see that site for its own terms and practices.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and post the revised policy at this same address. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
